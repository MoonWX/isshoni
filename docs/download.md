# Desktop apps

**Coming.** The desktop apps share your screen with all of your computer's sound except the voice apps (Discord,
TeamSpeak and the like), with hardware video encoding. They come one platform at a time:

| App | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 22H2 and later | M2 |
| macOS 14.4 and later | M3 |
| Linux share agent (Ubuntu 22.04 and later) | M4 |

Native iPhone and Android apps are pending; phones watch in the browser, and can add isshoni to the Home Screen
([Guide for friends](/guide/#phone)).

Until the apps are here, share from Chrome or Edge on a computer ([Share](/guide/#share)). The roadmap is in the
[project plan](https://github.com/MoonWX/isshoni/blob/main/docs/PLAN.md#roadmap-solo-developer-the-estimates-are-rough).
