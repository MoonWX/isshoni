# Guide for friends

Someone in your group runs an isshoni server and sent you an invite link. This page is for you. You need nothing but
a current browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer or a phone.

## Join {#join}

1. Open the invite link. Pick a username and a password: that's your account on this server.
2. You land in the room **Lounge**, where everyone meets. If someone is sharing, their screen plays right away.
3. Tap or click **once** to turn the sound on: browsers start videos muted until you do.

**Opened from a chat app?** Use its ⋯ menu → Open in Safari/Chrome first. A chat app's built-in browser keeps its own
cookies, so you would have to log in again in your real browser, and it can't add isshoni to your Home Screen.
isshoni shows a note when it recognizes such a browser.

## Watch {#watch}

- Every share is a tile. Click or tap one to **focus** it: it gets the full quality and the big place; the others
  stay small and light. The most recent share is focused automatically.
- **Sound follows focus**: you hear the focused share only. The other tiles have a speaker button to listen to them
  instead.
- **Fullscreen**: the button on the focused share, the `F` key, or a double-click or double-tap on it. On an iPhone
  the share opens in the phone's own video player.
- Your connection decides the quality: on a slow network a share comes in lighter and sharpens again by itself.
- Content protected by a streaming service (Netflix and the like) shows as a black picture in a share: their
  protection blocks screen capture.

## Share {#share}

Sharing works from **Chrome or Edge on a computer**. Click **Share**, and pick what to share in the browser's window:

- **A window, with its audio** is the best choice: friends see that window and hear only that app, not your voice
  app. Window audio needs Windows 11, or macOS 14.2 or later, with a current Chrome or Edge.
- **A browser tab** shares that tab, and its sound when you tick the browser's box for tab audio.
- **The whole screen with its sound** sends everything you hear, **including your voice app**, so friends would hear
  themselves. isshoni warns you before you start one.

The desktop apps for Windows and macOS, which share your computer's sound without the voice apps, come later
([Desktop apps](/download)). Firefox and Safari can watch but not share for now; phones can watch only.

## On a phone {#phone}

- **Add isshoni to your Home Screen** for an app of its own and for notifications ("Alex started sharing").
  - **iPhone and iPad** (iOS 16.4 or later): open the page in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open
    isshoni from the Home Screen and turn notifications on there. You log in once more inside the app: Home Screen
    apps keep their own cookies.
  - **Android**: in Chrome, ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
- **iPhone and iPad play only while isshoni is in front**: the video pauses when you switch apps or lock the screen,
  and comes back when you return. Sound may need one more tap then.
- Android keeps playing the sound in the background.

## When something doesn't work {#help}

- **"Test my connection"** in the account menu checks whether video can reach you, and gives you a result to copy
  and send to whoever runs the server.
- **The first visit with Firefox** can take about a minute before video plays: Firefox downloads its H.264 video
  decoder in the background the first time. If video still doesn't play after a few minutes, reload the page.
- More in [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).
